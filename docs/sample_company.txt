## Company Overview

NovaTech Solutions GmbH is a fictional technology company that provides software solutions for small and medium-sized businesses having around 10k employees. The company specializes in cloud applications, business process automation, data analytics, and artificial intelligence.

NovaTech's goal is to help organizations reduce manual work, improve operational efficiency, and make better business decisions using modern technology.

## Customer Data Policy

Customer information must only be accessed by employees who require the information to perform their job responsibilities.

Employees must not:

* Share customer information with unauthorized people.
* Store confidential information on personal devices.
* Send sensitive information through unauthorized communication channels.
* Copy production customer data into development environments.
* Share passwords or authentication tokens.

Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents to the IT Security Team.

## Employee Access Levels

Administrator:
Full system administration privileges.

Manager:
Access to department-level reports, team management, and operational information.

Employee:
Access to applications and information required for their assigned role.

External Contractor:
Limited access to specific systems and projects based on contractual requirements.

Support Agent:
Access to customer support systems and approved customer information.

## Document Classification

Public:
Information that can be shared publicly.

Internal:
Information intended for employees and authorized contractors.

Confidential:
Sensitive business information that must only be accessed by authorized employees.

Restricted:
Highly sensitive information requiring special authorization.

Example confidential information:

* Business contracts
* Financial forecasts
* Customer agreements
* Internal pricing
* Product roadmaps

Example restricted information:

* Production credentials
* Encryption keys
* Security incident details
* Personal employee information

## IT Support Process

Step 1:
Employee creates a support ticket.

Step 2:
Support team reviews the ticket and assigns a priority.

Step 3:
The ticket is assigned to an appropriate technical team.

Step 4:
The technical team investigates the issue.

Step 5:
A solution is provided or the issue is escalated.

Step 6:
The employee confirms whether the issue has been resolved.

Step 7:
The ticket is closed and documented.

## Ticket Priority Levels

Critical:
Complete service outage or major security incident.

High:
Major functionality unavailable for multiple users.

Medium:
Important functionality affected but a workaround exists.

Low:
General question, minor issue, or feature request.

## Incident Response

In the event of a security or technology incident:

1. Identify the incident.
2. Create an incident record.
3. Determine severity.
4. Contain the affected systems.
5. Investigate the root cause.
6. Remove the threat or resolve the issue.
7. Restore affected services.
8. Monitor systems.
9. Document the incident.
10. Conduct a post-incident review.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely when approved by their manager.

Remote employees must:

* Use company-approved devices.
* Use secure internet connections.
* Enable multi-factor authentication.
* Keep company devices physically secure.
* Avoid accessing confidential information from public computers.
* Report lost or stolen devices immediately.

## Password Policy

Employees must use strong and unique passwords.

Passwords should:

* Contain at least 12 characters.
* Not be shared with other employees.
* Not be reused across important systems.
* Not contain easily guessed personal information.

Multi-factor authentication is required for critical company systems.

## Backup Policy

Critical company systems are backed up automatically.

Backup requirements:

* Daily backups for critical databases.
* Weekly full system backups.
* Encrypted backup storage.
* Regular backup restoration tests.
* Restricted access to backup systems.

## Data Retention

Company records should be retained according to business, legal, and regulatory requirements.

Employees should not retain unnecessary personal or confidential information.

Expired information should be securely deleted or archived according to company policy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How can I reset my password?

A: Use the password reset option on the company login page. If the problem continues, contact Customer Support.

Q: How can I contact technical support?

A: Customers can contact support through the customer portal, email, phone, or online chat.

Q: How often is company data backed up?

A: Critical systems are backed up daily, with additional backup procedures depending on the system.

Q: Who can access confidential documents?

A: Only authorized employees who require access for their job responsibilities.

Q: What should I do if I suspect a security incident?

A: Report the issue immediately to the IT Security Team and avoid attempting to investigate or modify affected systems unless instructed to do so.

Q: Can employees work remotely?

A: Remote work may be available depending on the employee's role and manager approval.
